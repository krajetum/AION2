`world-map.webp` is the AION 2 world map (Asmodian), stitched from in-game screenshots (2898 × 3498 px).
`elyos-map.webp` is the Elyos map (Verteron), from an in-game screenshot (3148 × 3124 px, 2026-10-25).
`abyss-map.webp` is the Abyss map, from an in-game screenshot (1725 × 1187 px, 2026-10-25).
`krao-cave-map.webp` is the Krao Cave dungeon map, from an in-game screenshot (1514 × 1171 px, 2026-11-22).
`urugugu-canyon-map.webp` is the Urugugu Canyon dungeon map, from an in-game screenshot (1583 × 1107 px, 2026-11-22).
`fire-temple-map.webp` is the Fire Temple dungeon map, from an in-game screenshot (1677 × 1274 px, 2026-11-22).
`draupnir-cave-map.webp` is the Draupnir dungeon map, two in-game screenshots joined (1226 × 1757 px, 2026-11-22).
`vakron-sky-island-map.webp` is the Vakron Sky Island dungeon map, three in-game screenshots joined (2400 × 2272 px, 2026-11-22).
